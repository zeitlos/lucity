title: Metrics

Lucity keeps track of how much CPU and memory each [service](/docs/services) uses, and how full each [volume](/docs/volumes) is.

## Service metrics

Open a service on the canvas and switch to the **Metrics** tab. It charts CPU and memory over the last hour, 6 hours, 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days.

![The Metrics tab of a service, charting CPU and memory over the last 30 days](/img/docs/metrics/service-metrics.png)

*CPU and memory over 30 days. The dashed line marks a deployment.*

**Total** adds up all replicas of the service, while **Per replica** splits the chart into one band per replica. The **max** next to each chart's title is the most the service can use, which is its CPU or memory limit times the number of replicas. Every deployment is marked with a dashed line, so you can tell whether a change moved the numbers. Hovering over a chart shows the exact values at that point in time, along with an **Edit** link to change the limit in the service settings, and [CPU and memory](/docs/services#cpu-and-memory) explains what happens when a service reaches it.

CPU is measured in cores, and anything below one core is shown in thousandths, so 250m is a quarter of a core. Metrics show up a minute or two after a service starts, and the charts refresh every 30 seconds.

## Volume usage

Open a volume and switch to the **Usage** tab to see how much of the disk is in use, as a percentage of its size and charted over the same time ranges.

On the canvas, a mounted volume is attached under its service and fills from left to right as the disk fills up. Usage is only recorded while the volume is mounted.
