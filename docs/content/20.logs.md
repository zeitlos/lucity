title: Logs
description: What your services print while they run, and the output of every deployment step.
---

There are two kinds of logs. Runtime logs are what a [service](/docs/services) prints while it runs, and deployment logs are the output of each step of a [deployment](/docs/deployments).

## Runtime logs

Open a service on the canvas and click **Logs** next to the **Deployments** tab. The logs open in a panel of their own and stream live.

![Runtime logs of a service streaming in a panel next to the canvas](/img/docs/logs/runtime-logs.png)

*A service's output as it starts up. Pause and clear sit in the top right.*

The panel starts with the last 1000 lines of each running replica and then follows along as new lines come in. When a service runs more than one replica, each line starts with a short ID of the replica that printed it.

The panel sticks with the replicas that were running when you opened it, so reopen it after a deployment to follow the new ones. If a service keeps crashing, the logs show what the last attempt printed before it exited.

## Deployment logs

Open the service's **Deployments** tab, expand a deployment and click **Logs** next to **Build**, **Secrets** or **Deploy**. The logs stream while the step runs and stay available for a week. After that, the deployment stays in the list but only shows its rollout.

![A deployment expanded in the Deployments tab, with a Logs button next to its Build, Secrets and Deploy steps](/img/docs/logs/deployment-logs.png)

*Build, Secrets and Deploy each keep their own logs.*

Rollout has no logs of its own. If a new version never becomes healthy, its [runtime logs](#runtime-logs) usually say why.
