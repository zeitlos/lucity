# Marketing & Content

For docs, site copy, and announcements.

- **Tone**: confident and friendly, like explaining something cool to a smart colleague. An occasional well-placed quip beats three forced ones.
- **Never use em dashes.** Use periods, commas, colons, semicolons, or parentheses.
- **The differentiator is ejectability**: no lock-in, standard tools, your infrastructure stays yours. Lead with it.
- **Credit the open source we build on** (Kubernetes, Helm, CloudNativePG, Zot, Gateway API, and the rest).
- Light playful shade at other PaaS providers is fine; the market is big enough for many. Punch with substance, not sneers.
- **Avoid**: buzzword soup, empty superlatives, walls of text, vague promises, generic stock-photo energy. Be specific about what the platform actually does.
